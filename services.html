STRUCTURAL BRIEFING

Clarifying the foundations of our process.

What is the average duration for a 2,400 sqft house? expand_more
For a standard G+1 structure of approximately 2,400 sqft, the construction timeline typically spans 12 to 14 months, ensuring all structural curing periods are strictly observed for maximum longevity.
Do you handle BBMP/BDA plan sanctions? expand_more
Yes, we provide end-to-face assistance for all statutory approvals, including BBMP and BDA sanctions, ensuring your building plan complies with the latest bylaws.
Which cement and steel brands do you use? expand_more
We exclusively use premium brands like UltraTech or ACC for cement, and TATA Tiscon or JSW Neosteel for structural reinforcement.
Is Vastu compliance included in the base plan? expand_more
Absolutely. Every plan we create is reviewed by Vastu experts to ensure optimal energy flow without compromising on modern functional design.
