Who we are

Started with

Founded by Mr. Harish S. in 2009, HS Construction began as a passionate response to Bengaluru's growing need for reliable, high-quality residential spaces. What started with a single small renovation project has blossomed into a full-scale construction firm known for its uncompromising standards.

Our growth has been organic, fueled by the recommendations of over 500 happy families who now call an HS-built structure their home. We don't just build walls; we create the framework for memories, ensuring every square foot meets the highest safety and design standards.
